The Sugar Rule

No added sugar. Nothing with sugar or any sugar equivalent in the ingredients, and nothing showing added sugar on the label. No fruit juice. Whole fruit and plain unsweetened dairy allowed. Check the label on anything packaged before it goes in your mouth.

What I Can Drink

  • Water — plain or with lemon, lime, cucumber, mint
  • Sparkling water — 0g sugar, no sweetener in ingredients
  • Coffee — black or with plain unsweetened milk
  • Tea — unsweetened, any kind
  • Plain unsweetened milk — dairy or plant (check the label)
  • Zero-sugar sodas — Coke Zero, Diet Coke, 0g sugar

Watch These

  • Fruit juice — even 100%, no added sugar. Eat the fruit, don't drink it.
  • Kombucha — usually has added sugar
  • Flavored plant milk — often sweetened
  • Anything labeled "beer," tonic, or lemonade — sugar hides here
